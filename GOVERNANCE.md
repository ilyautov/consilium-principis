# Governance

Consilium Principis is maintained by its repository maintainers. Maintainers review contributions,
resolve routine issues, and protect the project's non-negotiable contracts: lawful source handling,
fail-closed fidelity, privacy boundaries, and reproducible tests.

Technical proposals should be discussed in an issue before substantial implementation. A maintainer
makes the final merge decision after considering evidence, tests, user impact, and compatibility.
For contested source markers, use the source-dispute form so the claim can be reproduced and audited.

Maintainers may update this governance document as the project grows. Security reports follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and participation expectations follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
